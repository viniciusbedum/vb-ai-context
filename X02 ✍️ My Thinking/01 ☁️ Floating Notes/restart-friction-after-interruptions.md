# Restart friction after interruptions

## Idea

I often blame an interruption for lost momentum, but the larger cost may come from leaving a task without a visible re-entry point. A short note containing the current state, next action, and open question might reduce that friction.

## Why it matters

This reframes continuity as something I can design before stopping, not something I must reconstruct later.
