# Small context packets make work easier to resume

## Claim

A task is easier to resume when I leave a small context packet at the moment I stop.

## Reasoning

At the stopping point, I still know the current state, the next concrete action, and the unresolved question. Recording those three items preserves the information most likely to be expensive to reconstruct later.

This does not require documenting the entire task. The packet only needs to reduce uncertainty at re-entry.

## Implications

Before pausing important work, I can write three lines: where it stands, what comes next, and what remains unclear. I should use this only when the expected restart cost is greater than the capture cost.
