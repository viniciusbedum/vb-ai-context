# Field Notes from the Cedar Lane Workshop

> This source is original fictional material created only to demonstrate VB AI Context. Mira Solis, Lantern Field Lab, Threadmap Cards, the workshop, and its results are fictional.

Lantern Field Lab invited twelve fictional participants to pause and resume a set of planning tasks over three afternoons. Facilitator Mira Solis asked half of the participants to leave a short note before each pause. The note contained three fields: current state, next action, and open question.

The other participants could leave any notes they wanted. Their notes often became either empty reminders or long chronological logs.

In the workshop discussion, participants using the three-field note reported that they could identify a re-entry action more quickly. The group called the note a “context packet.” They used paper Threadmap Cards to keep the exercise visible, but Solis argued that the medium was less important than capturing the state before attention moved elsewhere.

The workshop was exploratory and did not test long-term performance. It did not establish that context packets improve every kind of work. Its practical suggestion was narrower: when a task will be interrupted and reconstruction is likely to be costly, leave a compact re-entry cue.
