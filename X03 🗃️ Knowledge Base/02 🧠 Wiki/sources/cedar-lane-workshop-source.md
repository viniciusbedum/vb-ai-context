# Field Notes from the Cedar Lane Workshop

## Summary

This fictional article describes an exploratory workshop in which participants paused and resumed planning tasks. Some participants left a three-field note containing the current state, next action, and open question. They called this a context packet and reported easier re-entry during the workshop.

The source does not establish a general productivity effect. Its evidence is limited to a small fictional workshop and participant reports.

## Key points

- a compact re-entry cue can preserve task state at an interruption;
- the proposed packet contains current state, next action, and open question;
- the medium is presented as less important than the timing and content;
- the workshop does not support broad causal claims.

## Operational use

Test a three-line packet on tasks that are expensive to reconstruct. Compare the capture effort with the actual reduction in restart friction.
