# Projects

This folder holds the user's own active, ongoing real-life projects — a job search, a business idea being validated, any real work that accumulates content and continues across sessions.

Put project files, findings, and status here. Do not put abstract thinking (that's `X02 ✍️ My Thinking/`) or light AI-written plans and specs (that's `Z04 🤖 AI Workspace/projects/`) here.

A project that grows past a single note gets its own subfolder with a `logs/` subfolder for session history — see "Project-scoped logs" in `AGENTS.md`.
