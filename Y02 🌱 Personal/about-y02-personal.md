# Personal

This folder holds personal notes that are neither abstract thinking developed into a durable claim (`X02 ✍️ My Thinking/`), a dated journal entry (`X05 📝 Daily Notes/`), nor an active project (`Y01 💼 Projects/`).

Put standalone personal information and notes here — anything you want kept that doesn't fit those three more specific homes.
