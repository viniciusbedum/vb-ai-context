# Decision

## Decision

Keep CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 as the project license, rewritten with a plain-language summary at the top followed by the full legal text — not switched to MIT.

## Context

The author wants free personal/professional use but no commercial resale. MIT permits resale outright, so it doesn't fit that requirement regardless of what other, more complex competing projects chose. The original CC BY-NC-SA file was the full ~400-line legalcode with no summary, which the author found confusing.

## Alternatives considered

- MIT — rejected: permits commercial resale, contradicts the stated requirement.
- CC BY-NC (drop ShareAlike) — considered, not chosen; ShareAlike was kept from the original choice.

## Consequences

LICENSE keeps GitHub's automatic license detection (full legalcode preserved) while staying readable at a glance via the summary block on top.
